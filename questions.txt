## Demo sequence (shows all four features in ~30 seconds)

Step 1 — Keyword mode:
  the process by which plants make food
  → likely 0 or few results

Step 2 — Semantic mode (same query):
  the process by which plants make food
  → returns science-photosynthesis-0001 and similar

Step 3 — Hybrid mode:
  photosynthesis
  → exact document at top, highlighted terms

Step 4 — Facet:
  click the "Science" chip
  → list narrows



## What won't work (and why)

What is 2+2?              → not in the corpus
Who won the World Cup?    → sports aren't in the synthetic data
the and of is             → all stopwords, nothing to match
asdfghjkl                 → no such term
Show me everything        → use facets to browse instead

Rule of thumb: if the topic is one of the subjects listed above,
or one of the history/geography pattern words, the query will work.
